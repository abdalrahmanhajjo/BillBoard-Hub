- Intent: commercial investigation

- Title: `How Much Does Billboard Advertising Cost in Lebanon?`

  1. Short answer explaining why there is no single price
  2. Cost factors: city, road, size, format, direction, traffic, duration
  3. Static production and installation costs
  4. Digital rotation, slot duration, and share of voice
  5. Example budget scenarios clearly labeled as examples
  6. What a Boardly listing rate includes and does not include
  7. Reservation and final-confirmation process

  - How much is a billboard in Beirut?
  - Are digital billboards more expensive than static?
  - Is printing included?
  - What campaign duration is most efficient?

## Editorial quality rules

- Verify all pricing, traffic, availability, and legal claims before publication.
- Distinguish estimated traffic from unique reach and delivered impressions.
- Do not describe pending reservation requests as confirmed bookings.
- Use real inventory images only with documented rights.
- Name an author and reviewer; include published and updated dates.
- Add contextual internal links, not generic “click here” links.
- Update high-intent commercial guides at least every six months.
