# Competitor and SERP analysis

Research date: 27 July 2026.

## Observed search landscape

Commercial searches are dominated by established media owners and agencies. Many competitors lead
with tenure, network size, nationwide coverage, proprietary formats, and request-a-quote flows.
Seif Media is the clearest marketplace-like result observed: its search result exposes individual
inventory, availability, dimensions, regions, and quote actions.

Boardly’s defensible search position is not “another outdoor advertising company.” It is:

> A searchable, structured marketplace for comparing verified static and digital billboard
> inventory, monthly traffic, dimensions, pricing, dates, and reservation status across Lebanon.

This distinction should stay visible in titles, copy, schema, and product functionality.

## Authority, backlinks, and Core Web Vitals

Domain Authority/Domain Rating and backlink counts are proprietary third-party metrics, not public
search-engine data. Field Core Web Vitals require Chrome UX Report data, which may be unavailable
for lower-traffic domains. No unsupported numbers are recorded in this audit.

Before outreach or a migration:

1. Export referring domains, top linked pages, anchor text, and lost links for all ten domains from
   Ahrefs, Semrush, or Moz.
2. Query PageSpeed Insights or CrUX for the homepage and one representative detail page on mobile
   and desktop.
3. Record LCP, INP, and CLS at the 75th percentile.
4. Re-run quarterly and after major frontend releases.

Current good thresholds are LCP at or below 2.5 seconds, INP at or below 200 milliseconds, and CLS
at or below 0.1 at the 75th percentile.

## Content gaps

- Transparent “how pricing works” guide for Lebanon
- City and corridor landing pages with enough unique inventory
- Static versus digital comparison with local operational details
- Traffic, reach, and impression methodology
- Creative specifications by actual screen/board format
- Verified case studies with objectives, placements, dates, and measured outcomes
- Media-owner onboarding and inventory-quality requirements
- Arabic or French demand validation before localization

## Backlink and outreach opportunities

Prioritize relevance and editorial value over volume:

1. Lebanese marketing and business publications covering media innovation
2. Advertising associations, agency partner pages, and campaign directories
3. Universities and marketing programs needing OOH planning resources
4. Chambers of commerce and startup ecosystems
5. Tourism, retail, events, and automotive trade publications
6. Media-owner partner profiles that link to their verified Boardly inventory
7. Data-led annual “Lebanon OOH inventory and traffic trends” reports

Recommended anchor mix:

- 50–60% branded or URL anchors
- 20–30% descriptive partial-match anchors
- 10–20% natural editorial anchors such as “this planning guide”
- Avoid repeated exact-match commercial anchors
